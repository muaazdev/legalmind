PROFESSIONAL SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Professional Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 1, 2024 ("Effective Date") by and between:

CLIENT: Acme Corporation, a Delaware corporation ("Client")
CONTRACTOR: TechServ Solutions LLC, a California limited liability company ("Contractor")

1. SCOPE OF SERVICES

1.1 The Contractor agrees to provide professional software development and consulting services as described in Exhibit A attached hereto (the "Services").

1.2 Services shall include but are not limited to:
    (a) Software architecture design and review
    (b) Application development and coding
    (c) Quality assurance and testing
    (d) Technical documentation
    (e) Training and knowledge transfer

2. COMPENSATION

2.1 Hourly Rate: The Client shall pay the Contractor at the rate of One Hundred Fifty Dollars ($150.00) per hour for Services rendered.

2.2 Expenses: The Client shall reimburse the Contractor for reasonable pre-approved expenses incurred in connection with the Services.

2.3 Invoicing: The Contractor shall submit invoices on a bi-weekly basis. Payment is due within thirty (30) days of invoice receipt.

2.4 Late Payment: Overdue amounts shall accrue interest at the rate of 1.5% per month.

3. TERM AND TERMINATION

3.1 Initial Term: This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for a period of twelve (12) months unless earlier terminated.

3.2 Termination for Convenience: Either party may terminate this Agreement with thirty (30) days prior written notice.

3.3 Termination for Cause: Either party may terminate immediately upon material breach that remains uncured for fifteen (15) days after written notice.

3.4 Effect of Termination: Upon termination, Client shall pay for all Services rendered through the termination date.

4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

4.1 Work Product: All work product created by Contractor specifically for Client under this Agreement shall be owned by Client.

4.2 Pre-Existing IP: Contractor retains ownership of all pre-existing intellectual property and general-purpose tools.

4.3 License: Contractor grants Client a perpetual, non-exclusive license to use any Contractor IP incorporated into deliverables.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 Definition: "Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by either party.

5.2 Obligations: Each party agrees to maintain the confidentiality of the other party's Confidential Information for a period of five (5) years.

5.3 Exceptions: Confidential Information does not include information that is publicly available or independently developed.

6. LIABILITY AND INDEMNIFICATION

6.1 Limitation of Liability: THE CONTRACTOR'S TOTAL LIABILITY SHALL NOT EXCEED THE TOTAL FEES PAID BY CLIENT IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS PRECEDING THE CLAIM.

6.2 Exclusion: IN NO EVENT SHALL EITHER PARTY BE LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES.

6.3 Indemnification: Contractor shall indemnify Client against third-party claims arising from Contractor's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

7. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

7.1 Authority: Each party represents that it has the authority to enter into this Agreement.

7.2 Services: Contractor warrants that Services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner.

7.3 No Conflicts: Contractor represents that performance of Services does not conflict with any other agreement.

8. INSURANCE

8.1 Contractor shall maintain:
    (a) Professional Liability Insurance: $2,000,000 per occurrence
    (b) General Liability Insurance: $1,000,000 per occurrence
    (c) Workers' Compensation: As required by law

9. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

9.1 Negotiation: The parties shall first attempt to resolve disputes through good faith negotiation.

9.2 Mediation: If negotiation fails, disputes shall be submitted to mediation in San Francisco, California.

9.3 Arbitration: If mediation fails, disputes shall be resolved by binding arbitration under AAA rules.

10. GOVERNING LAW

10.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of California, without regard to conflicts of law principles.

10.2 Venue: Any litigation shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in San Francisco County, California.

11. MISCELLANEOUS

11.1 Entire Agreement: This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties.

11.2 Amendments: This Agreement may only be amended in writing signed by both parties.

11.3 Assignment: Neither party may assign this Agreement without prior written consent.

11.4 Force Majeure: Neither party shall be liable for delays caused by circumstances beyond reasonable control, including natural disasters, war, terrorism, or government actions.

11.5 Severability: If any provision is found unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in effect.

11.6 Notices: All notices shall be in writing and delivered to the addresses set forth below.
