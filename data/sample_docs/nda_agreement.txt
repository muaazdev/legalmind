NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Non-Disclosure Agreement ("NDA") is entered into as of February 15, 2024, by and between:

DISCLOSING PARTY: Acme Corporation ("Discloser")
RECEIVING PARTY: Innovation Partners Inc. ("Recipient")

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Discloser possesses certain confidential and proprietary information relating to its business operations, technology, and strategic plans;

WHEREAS, Recipient desires to receive such information for the purpose of evaluating a potential business relationship;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, the parties agree as follows:

1. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

1.1 "Confidential Information" shall mean any and all information disclosed by Discloser to Recipient, whether oral, written, or electronic, including but not limited to:

    (a) Trade secrets, inventions, and patent applications
    (b) Software code, algorithms, and technical specifications
    (c) Business plans, strategies, and financial projections
    (d) Customer lists, pricing information, and marketing plans
    (e) Employee information and organizational charts
    (f) Any information marked as "Confidential" or "Proprietary"

1.2 Confidential Information shall not include information that:
    (a) Is or becomes publicly available through no fault of Recipient
    (b) Was known to Recipient prior to disclosure
    (c) Is independently developed by Recipient without use of Confidential Information
    (d) Is disclosed to Recipient by a third party with the right to disclose

2. OBLIGATIONS OF RECIPIENT

2.1 Non-Disclosure: Recipient shall not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without prior written consent of Discloser.

2.2 Limited Use: Recipient shall use Confidential Information solely for the purpose of evaluating a potential business relationship.

2.3 Protection: Recipient shall protect Confidential Information using the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential information, but no less than reasonable care.

2.4 Access Limitation: Recipient shall limit access to Confidential Information to employees who have a need to know and who are bound by confidentiality obligations.

3. PERMITTED DISCLOSURES

3.1 Legal Requirement: Recipient may disclose Confidential Information if required by law, provided that Recipient:
    (a) Gives Discloser prompt notice of such requirement
    (b) Cooperates with Discloser in seeking protective measures
    (c) Discloses only the minimum information required

4. RETURN OF INFORMATION

4.1 Upon termination of this NDA or upon Discloser's request, Recipient shall:
    (a) Return all Confidential Information in tangible form
    (b) Destroy all copies, notes, and derivatives
    (c) Certify in writing that all materials have been returned or destroyed

5. NO LICENSE OR WARRANTY

5.1 No License: This NDA does not grant Recipient any license or rights to Confidential Information.

5.2 No Warranty: Confidential Information is provided "AS IS" without warranty of any kind.

5.3 No Obligation: This NDA does not obligate either party to enter into any business relationship.

6. TERM AND TERMINATION

6.1 Term: This NDA shall remain in effect for three (3) years from the Effective Date.

6.2 Survival: The confidentiality obligations shall survive termination for a period of five (5) years.

7. REMEDIES

7.1 Injunctive Relief: Recipient acknowledges that breach of this NDA may cause irreparable harm. Discloser shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief without posting bond.

7.2 Monetary Damages: Discloser may also seek monetary damages for any breach of this NDA.

7.3 Attorney's Fees: The prevailing party in any dispute shall be entitled to recover reasonable attorney's fees.

8. GENERAL PROVISIONS

8.1 Governing Law: This NDA shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

8.2 Entire Agreement: This NDA constitutes the entire agreement regarding confidentiality.

8.3 Amendment: This NDA may only be amended in writing signed by both parties.

8.4 Assignment: Neither party may assign this NDA without prior written consent.

8.5 Severability: Invalid provisions shall be modified to be enforceable to the maximum extent permitted.

8.6 Counterparts: This NDA may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be an original.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this NDA as of the date first written above.
